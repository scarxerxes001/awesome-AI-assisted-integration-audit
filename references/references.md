# Verified Scholarly References

> Topic: **Benchmarking Factual Accuracy of Large Language Models Across Scientific Domains**

The entries below were checked against publisher, ACL Anthology, arXiv, or other authoritative scholarly records. Links point to the paper record or persistent identifier.

## 1. TruthfulQA: Measuring How Models Mimic Human Falsehoods

**Authors:** Stephanie Lin, Jacob Hilton, Owain Evans  
**Year / Venue:** 2022, Proceedings of ACL  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2022.acl-long.229  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.229/

**Relevance:** Introduces a benchmark for measuring whether language models produce truthful answers to questions designed around common misconceptions.

## 2. Fact or Fiction: Verifying Scientific Claims

**Authors:** David Wadden, Shanchuan Lin, Kyle Lo, Lucy Lu Wang, Madeleine van Zuylen, Arman Cohan, Hannaneh Hajishirzi  
**Year / Venue:** 2020, EMNLP  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2020.emnlp-main.609  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.609/

**Relevance:** Introduces SciFact for verifying scientific claims against evidence-bearing scientific abstracts.

## 3. SciFact-Open: Towards Open-Domain Scientific Claim Verification

**Authors:** David Wadden, Kyle Lo, Bailey Kuehl, Arman Cohan, Iz Beltagy, Lucy Lu Wang, Hannaneh Hajishirzi  
**Year / Venue:** 2022, Findings of EMNLP  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2022.findings-emnlp.347  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2022.findings-emnlp.347/

**Relevance:** Extends scientific claim verification to a large collection of research abstracts, making it useful for realistic evidence retrieval and verification.

## 4. FActScore: Fine-grained Atomic Evaluation of Factual Precision in Long Form Text Generation

**Authors:** Sewon Min, Kalpesh Krishna, Xinxi Lyu, Mike Lewis, Wen-tau Yih, Pang Koh, Mohit Iyyer, Luke Zettlemoyer, Hannaneh Hajishirzi  
**Year / Venue:** 2023, EMNLP  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.741  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/

**Relevance:** Decomposes long-form model output into atomic facts and measures the percentage supported by a reliable knowledge source.

## 5. SelfCheckGPT: Zero-Resource Black-Box Hallucination Detection for Generative Large Language Models

**Authors:** Potsawee Manakul, Adian Liusie, Mark Gales  
**Year / Venue:** 2023, EMNLP  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.557  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.557/

**Relevance:** Presents a black-box hallucination detection method based on consistency across multiple sampled responses.

## 6. HaluEval: A Large-Scale Hallucination Evaluation Benchmark for Large Language Models

**Authors:** Junyi Li, Xiaoxue Cheng, Xin Zhao, Jian-Yun Nie, Ji-Rong Wen  
**Year / Venue:** 2023, EMNLP  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.397  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.397/

**Relevance:** Provides a large benchmark of generated and human-annotated hallucinated samples for evaluating hallucination recognition.

## 7. FELM: Benchmarking Factuality Evaluation of Large Language Models

**Authors:** Shiqi Chen, Yiran Zhao, Jinghan Zhang, I-Chun Chern, Siyang Gao, Pengfei Liu, Junxian He  
**Year / Venue:** 2023, NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks Track  
**Paper:** https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/8b8a7960d343e023a6a0afe37eee6022-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html

**Relevance:** Evaluates factuality across diverse domains, including world knowledge, mathematics, and reasoning, with fine-grained annotations.

## 8. Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations

**Authors:** Tianyu Gao, Howard Yen, Jiatong Yu, Danqi Chen  
**Year / Venue:** 2023, EMNLP  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.398  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/

**Relevance:** Introduces ALCE, a benchmark for evaluating LLM-generated answers together with supporting citations.

## 9. Beyond Factuality: A Comprehensive Evaluation of Large Language Models as Knowledge Generators

**Authors:** Liang Chen, Yang Deng, Yatao Bian, Zeyu Qin, Bingzhe Wu, Tat-Seng Chua, Kam-Fai Wong  
**Year / Venue:** 2023, EMNLP  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.390  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.390/

**Relevance:** Introduces CONNER, which evaluates generated knowledge across factuality and several complementary dimensions.

## 10. TRUE: Re-evaluating Factual Consistency Evaluation

**Authors:** Or Honovich, Roee Aharoni, Jonathan Herzig, Hagai Taitelbaum, Doron Kukliansy, Vered Cohen, Thomas Scialom, Idan Szpektor, Avinatan Hassidim, Yossi Matias  
**Year / Venue:** 2022, NAACL-HLT  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2022.naacl-main.287  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2022.naacl-main.287/

**Relevance:** Provides a standardized meta-evaluation of factual-consistency metrics across multiple tasks and datasets.

## 11. Asking and Answering Questions to Evaluate the Factual Consistency of Summaries

**Authors:** Alex Wang, Kyunghyun Cho, Mike Lewis  
**Year / Venue:** 2020, ACL  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2020.acl-main.450  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.450/

**Relevance:** Introduces QAGS, which compares answers to questions generated from a summary and its source to detect factual inconsistencies.

## 12. Evaluating the Factual Consistency of Abstractive Text Summarization

**Authors:** Wojciech Kryscinski, Bryan McCann, Caiming Xiong, Richard Socher  
**Year / Venue:** 2020, EMNLP  
**DOI:** 10.18653/v1/2020.emnlp-main.750  
**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.750/

**Relevance:** Presents FactCC for factual-consistency verification of abstractive summaries and releases data and model resources.

## 13. RAGTruth: A Hallucination Corpus for Developing Trustworthy Retrieval-Augmented Language Models

**Authors:** Cheng Niu, Yuanhao Wu, Juno Zhu, Siliang Xu, KaShun Shum, Randy Zhong, Juntong Song, Tong Zhang

**Paper:** https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.585/

## 14. FactBench: A Dynamic Benchmark for In-the-Wild Language Model Factuality Evaluation

**Year / Venue:** 2025, ACL

## 15. OpenFactCheck: Building, Benchmarking Customized Fact-Checking Systems and Evaluating the Factuality of Claims and LLMs
