## Overview

Large Language Models can generate fluent and convincing answers across scientific and technical domains, but fluency does not necessarily guarantee factual accuracy. This repository focuses on the evaluation and benchmarking of factual accuracy in LLM-generated scientific information.

The project began with an AI-assisted research paper generated for the assigned topic **“Benchmarking Factual Accuracy of Large Language Models Across Scientific Domains.”** The original paper and its citation-integrity audit are preserved as part of the research record. The audit examines whether cited publications exist and whether their bibliographic details correspond to credible scholarly records.

The repository then extends the initial paper into a curated research resource. It is intended to collect verified scholarly literature, relevant datasets, tools and libraries, reproducible implementations, and learning resources related to factuality evaluation, hallucination detection, scientific question answering, and benchmarking of language models.

The aim is to make the material easier to inspect, reproduce, and extend while keeping a clear distinction between the original AI-generated work and independently curated research resources.
