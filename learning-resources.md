# Tutorials and Learning Resources

- **[Stanford CS324: Large Language Models](https://stanford-cs324.github.io/winter2022/)** - University course material covering capabilities, risks, and evaluation.
- **[Hugging Face LLM Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter1/1)** - Practical introduction to transformer and LLM workflows.
- **[NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)** - Authoritative risk-management guidance relevant to testing and deployment.
- **[MLCommons AI Safety Benchmark](https://mlcommons.org/benchmarks/ai-safety/)** - Community benchmark initiative for measuring AI safety-related behaviours.
- **[ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/)** - Authoritative searchable archive of computational-linguistics publications.
- **[arXiv submission and access guide](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit.html)** - Reference for locating and citing openly available preprints responsibly.
