# Datasets and Benchmarks

- **SciFact** - [Dataset and code](https://github.com/allenai/scifact) | Scientific claims, abstracts, and evidence rationales. Use it to evaluate evidence retrieval and support/refute/neutral classification.
- **PubMedQA** - [Official project](https://pubmedqa.github.io/) | Biomedical yes/no/maybe questions paired with PubMed abstracts and reasoning.
- **MedQA (USMLE)** - [Dataset repository](https://github.com/jind11/MedQA) | Multiple-choice medical exam questions. Use it to measure clinical knowledge and reasoning, with caution that exam accuracy alone is not clinical safety.
- **GPQA** - [Dataset repository](https://github.com/idavidrein/gpqa) | Expert-written graduate questions in biology, physics, and chemistry. Use it for demanding scientific reasoning evaluation.
- **MMLU** - [Dataset repository](https://github.com/hendrycks/test) | Multitask questions across 57 subjects, including STEM and professional medicine. Use it for broad comparative reporting.
- **ARC** - [AI2 ARC](https://allenai.org/data/arc) | Grade-school science questions split into easy and challenge sets. Use it for scientific reasoning baselines.

All data must be used under the source project's stated terms and with the appropriate train/test protocol.
